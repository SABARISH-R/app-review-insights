How I work with a client

  1. Scope the brief. I meet the client, understand the need, and decide what is worth building.
  2. Build with specialists. Requirements become working product modules, reviewed with people who know the subject.
  3. Test with real users. Users try each module while I watch, listen and take notes.
  4. Train and upgrade. I train the client on the product, then upgrade it from user-experience feedback.
